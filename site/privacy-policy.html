Privacy Policy

FlashEx is designed to help users turn natural-language hardware ideas into ready-to-run Arduino project scaffolds. We respect your privacy and keep our data processing transparent.

1. Information we process

We may process technical and operational data necessary to improve the product, including:

  • Project names and prompt text you provide to generate the hardware setup
  • Runtime environment information needed to diagnose setup issues
  • Browser or application metadata required for installability and compatibility checks
  • Feedback, crash reports, or issue details if you choose to share them

2. How we use information

We use this information to:

  • Generate project scaffolds and dependency guidance
  • Improve installation reliability and compatibility
  • Diagnose bugs, prevent abuse, and maintain service quality
  • Provide the documentation, product updates, and support channels associated with FlashEx

3. Data sharing

FlashEx does not sell personal data. We may share limited information with trusted service providers when necessary for hosting, analytics, support, or security operations. We only share what is required to operate the service responsibly.

4. Cookies and local storage

FlashEx may use browser storage to remember user preferences such as theme selection and installation-related state. This data stays on the device and is not used for invasive tracking.

5. Your choices

You may disable browser storage, clear local preferences, or avoid submitting optional diagnostics. If you do not wish to share prompt content with a third-party AI provider, you may choose not to use those features.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical safeguards to protect user data from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no system is completely immune to security risks.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the project maintainers through the public GitHub repository or project support channels.
